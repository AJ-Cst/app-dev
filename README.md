## Overview
*Project Hail Mary* is a **2026 sci-fi adventure film** directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller and based on the bestselling novel by Andy Weir, the author of *The Martian*. A science teacher wakes up alone on a spaceship, light years from home, with no memory of who he is or how he got there.

## Central Conflict
Ryland Grace has to piece together his lost memories and use his scientific knowledge to save **all life on Earth** from extinction. Along the way, he meets an unexpected friend who might help him solve the problem.

## Main Characters
- **Ryland Grace** (Ryan Gosling): a science teacher turned astronaut
- **Eva Stratt** (Sandra Hüller): a key figure behind the mission
- **Rocky**: an alien Grace meets in space, performed by James Ortiz
